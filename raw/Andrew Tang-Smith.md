## About

Andrew is a founding director of STUDIO TANGARA, a progressive studio led Architecture and Interior design practice based in Australia with projects in Perth, across regional WA and in China.  
  
Our website:  
https://studiotangara.com/  
  
He was formerly head of design as an Associate Director at Hames Sharley and Associate Principal at Woods Bagot. Building on an expertise of large mixed use, residential, workplace and aviation projects he has developed over 15 years of local and international professional experience through working on a variety of significant national and global projects across Australia, China and Dubai.  
  
He's experience in leading the design team on numerous cross-sector projects, driving design concepts including competitions and taking projects to completion. With an attention to detail, passion for unconventional creativity and drive to succeed is shown in both his extensive list of project experience and the awards he has been recognised with.  
  
Find me on:  
https://www.instagram.com/tangerz/
