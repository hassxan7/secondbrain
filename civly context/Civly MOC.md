# Civly — Map of Content

AI BIM copilot. Sketch, CAD, or Rhino in; coordinated, code-compliant, editable BIM out in under 20 minutes. Framed internally as "Cursor, but for civil engineering" (Master Info doc).

## The one thing to keep straight
Civly makes **engineering** decisions, never **design** decisions. The architect and client author the design; Civly evaluates it. Full reasoning in [[Civly - Design vs Engineering Decisions]].
