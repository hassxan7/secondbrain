# CIVLY — LAUNCH FILM: STORYBOARD + VO
### Demo-led hero film · ~55s · 16:9 LinkedIn · magic-first

**The build:** three layers doing distinct jobs —
- 🎬 **FOOTAGE** = the real Civly walkthrough (always framed/punched-in/ramped, never raw)
- ✦ **MOTION** = 2D After Effects motion graphics (metaphor, kinetic type, prompt-bar motif, callouts, lockup)
- ⬡ **BUILT** = the constructed blank→populated floorplan set piece (animated by us, not from footage)

**Recurring motif:** the **prompt bar** — type → enter → building reacts. It's the heartbeat of the edit and the literal "Cursor for architects" idea.

**Read-with-sound-off rule:** on-screen text carries meaning even muted; VO is the bonus layer for unmuted viewers.

## THE STORYBOARD

### MOVEMENT 1 — COLD OPEN / THE MAGIC (0:00–0:07) · no VO
*Goal: "what did I just see." Earn the right to explain.*

**SCENE 1.1 (0:00–0:03)** — ✦ MOTION + 🎬 FOOTAGE
- **On screen:** Black. A single CAD image of a building (the real input from the walkthrough) sits centered, small, like a dropped file. A soft amber scan-line passes over it.
- **Action:** the flat image *lifts* — edges extrude, and a wireframe begins to bloom out of it.
- **Text:** none. Maybe a faint cursor blink.
- **Sound:** deep pad swell, a single soft "scan" tick.

**SCENE 1.2 (0:03–0:07)** — 🎬 FOOTAGE (speed-ramped) → ✦ title
- **On screen:** hard cut to the walkthrough's hero moment — the **full BIM model + internal MEP materializing** from that CAD input. Speed-ramped: fast bloom, then slow-mo settle as it completes.
- **Action:** model finishes; everything holds for a half-beat.
- **Text:** title punches in over the held model — **CIVLY** (wordmark) / small sub: *Engineer in a Box.*
- **Sound:** the bloom rises into a satisfying low "resolve" hit on the title.

> *Edit note: this is your scroll-stopper. The first 2 seconds must show the transformation, not a logo. Logo comes AFTER the magic.*

### MOVEMENT 2 — THE METAPHOR / THE WHY (0:07–0:18) · VO begins
*Goal: make them care. Pure 2D motion graphics — type and shape, zero footage.*

**SCENE 2.1 (0:07–0:11)** — ✦ MOTION
- **On screen:** five simple rounded "rooms" appear on black, each with a tiny figure + a fragment of the same floorplan. Amber line-art style.
- **Action:** a small character icon moves in room one ("moves to Paris"); the other four rooms scramble to redraw — dashed lines try to sync between rooms and *snap*.
- **Text:** **ONE NOVEL. FIVE SEPARATE ROOMS.**
- **VO:** *"Designing a building is like writing one novel in five separate rooms."*

**SCENE 2.2 (0:11–0:16)** — ✦ MOTION
- **On screen:** the five rooms relabel as disciplines (Architect · Structural · Mechanical · Electrical · Plumbing). Connector lines between them flicker, misalign, turn red at the joins.
- **Action:** a red contradiction marker pulses where two systems clash.
- **Text:** **5 ENGINEERS. 1 STRUCTURE. EVERY CHANGE, BY HAND.**
- **VO:** *"Five engineers. One structure. Every change has to ripple through all of them — by hand."*

**SCENE 2.3 (0:16–0:18)** — ✦ MOTION
- **On screen:** the whole coordinated grid stutters and a crack of red runs through it; it darkens.
- **Text:** **MORE COORDINATING THAN DESIGNING.** → quick beat: **AND IT STILL BREAKS.**
- **VO:** *"They spend more time coordinating than designing. And projects still break at construction."*

### MOVEMENT 3 — THE TURN / CIVLY ENTERS (0:18–0:27) · the prompt bar arrives
*Goal: introduce Civly as the connective tissue + prove "it learns you" with the built floorplan.*

**SCENE 3.1 (0:18–0:21)** — ✦ MOTION
- **On screen:** the red mess collapses inward to a single point; from it, a clean amber **prompt bar** draws itself onto black — the hero UI element, centered.
- **Action:** cursor blinks in the empty bar.
- **Text:** the line **CIVLY** fades in above the bar.
- **VO:** *"So we built Civly."*

**SCENE 3.2 (0:21–0:27)** — ⬡ BUILT (the constructed set piece)
- **On screen:** behind the prompt bar, a **blank office floorplan outline** appears (just the perimeter). A line types in the bar: *"Lay out a 12-person office, code-compliant."*
- **Action:** on **enter**, the floorplan populates in a confident sequence — interior walls slide in → structural grid drops → desks/rooms fill → MEP routing threads through → a green "compliant" tick. Each step staggered, elegant, fast-but-legible.
- **Text:** small callout tags appear as each layer lands: *walls · structure · services · compliant.* Then: **IT LEARNS FROM YOUR PAST PROJECTS.**
- **VO:** *"Design naturally. Civly handles the rest."*

> *This is the moment that sells "trained on your data." It's constructed so it's clean and on-message — and it rhymes visually with the real footage coming next.*

### MOVEMENT 4 — THE PROOF / REAL DEMO (0:27–0:49) · the walkthrough, edited as hero
*Goal: credibility. This is real software. Footage carries it; motion directs the eye.*

**SCENE 4.1 (0:27–0:31)** — 🎬 FOOTAGE (framed)
- **On screen:** the walkthrough enters — but **floated in a dark cinematic frame**, not full-bleed. Left pane ArchiCAD, right pane the Civly agent. Gentle parallax/tilt so it feels 3D, soft amber glow behind the window.
- **Action:** the CAD image (building photo) is dropped into the input. Punch-in on the moment of import.
- **Text:** callout: **INPUT: CAD · SKETCH · MODEL**
- **VO:** *"Drop in a sketch, a CAD file, or a model…"*

**SCENE 4.2 (0:31–0:37)** — 🎬 FOOTAGE (the centerpiece drop, speed-ramped)
- **On screen:** the cold-start magic in full — CAD → **fully editable BIM with internal MEP**, auto-generated. Ramp: accelerate through the build, slow-mo on completion. Push in slightly as it finishes.
- **Action:** the finished model holds; a slow reveal of the internal MEP threading through.
- **Text:** callouts name the engine as it works: **MeshBridge → BIM** · **MEP auto-routed** · then **NCC compliant ✓**
- **VO:** *"…and watch it become a fully editable BIM — structure, systems, and all."*

> *This is the second and biggest drop. Let the completion breathe ~1s with no callout before the green tick lands.*

**SCENE 4.3 (0:37–0:43)** — 🎬 FOOTAGE (the prompt-bar rhythm)
- **On screen:** tight punch-in on the **right pane prompt bar**. A line is typed → enter → the building reacts in the left/centre. Repeat the beat 2–3x, cutting on each "enter."
- **Action:** *"make windows"* → windows appear. *"make doors"* → doors appear. *"route MEP"* → services thread.
- **Text:** each prompt shown as clean typed text (overlay-clean, in case footage text is rough): **"make windows" · "make doors" · "route the MEP"**
- **VO:** *"Just ask. Make the windows. Route the MEP. Civly proposes every decision—"*

**SCENE 4.4 (0:43–0:49)** — 🎬 FOOTAGE + ✦ MOTION (human-in-the-loop)
- **On screen:** a decision/approve interaction — a proposed change highlighted, then approved (green confirm). If footage of this is rough, cover with a clean motion-graphic approve card composited over the frame.
- **Action:** approve tap → green ripple → model updates.
- **Text:** **YOU APPROVE EVERY DECISION.** small: *trained on your projects · compliant by default*
- **VO:** *"—and you approve it. Trained on your past projects, compliant by default."*

> *Edit recipe for this whole movement: never show the raw desktop full-screen. Keep it floated + glowing, punch into hero regions, ramp the slow parts, and let callouts/the approve card sit over any rough UI patches.*

- **Text:** **2 MONTHS → 2 DAYS. 30× FASTER. 5× CHEAPER.**

- **On screen:** the wireframe building compresses into the Civly cube mark, centered, calm amber glow.
- **Text:** **Civly** wordmark + cube → tagline *Make the design. Leave the rest to us.* → amber button: **Join the waitlist →**
- **VO:** *"Civly. Make the design. Leave the rest to us."*
