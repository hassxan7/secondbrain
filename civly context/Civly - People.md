## Michael Westerlund — unofficial design partner

> [!warning] Do not call him a formal advisor or partner in anything public
> An investor can check with him. If he is contacted and the framing is overstated, it costs more than the name buys. Describe the relationship truthfully until he formalises it.

**Who he is:** architect behind the Sydney Quay Quarter Tower and the New Sydney Fish Market. Project manager at 3XN/GXN Copenhagen, former regional BIM manager at Semren and Mansson, founder of Sonder Studios.

**The real status (reconciled):**
- 22 Apr meeting: agreed informally. Asked to come on as a strategic advisor, he said "Yeah, sure, sure. I am happy to." Offered cadence around bi-weekly: "I'm easy, you know, you can always just ask me."
- The pitch deck and Master Info doc then hardened this into "design advisor and product partner."
- He walked that back in a recent message (~6 Jun, analysed in `info pt2`): he "hasn't had a chance to review the product or the work properly," "doesn't feel comfortable lending his name formally at this point," is open to informal exchanges, and would consider formalising "if there's a mentor group involved."

**Bottom line:** warm, informal, conditional. "Unofficial design partner, hoping he joins officially" is accurate. He did not refuse the relationship, he declined the formal label for now.

**His key contributions:**
- The early-design feasibility evaluation ask. See [[Civly - Design vs Engineering Decisions]].
- The BOM insight: the hard problem is consistency and traceability through continuous design iterations across disciplines, not initial quantity extraction.
- Branding advice: find the WHY before the what (Apple's "things that simply work"); the product evolves but the purpose stays constant.
- Pointed at Finch and Legora as companies to study, and confirmed IFC is unavoidable.
