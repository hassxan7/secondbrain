# Civly — Messaging Rules and Open Questions

Up: [[Civly MOC]]

## Messaging rules (do / don't)
- No em dashes in any copy.
- Frame Civly as the "AI copilot for architects / fastest way to a BIM draft," not a "coordination tool."
- Say "reads the design," not "reads your design intent."
- Avoid "automatically generates a complete coordinated BIM." Too stacked, reads as AI slop.
- Show control through the approval mechanic. Do not say "you stay in control."
- Do not compete with ACC on clash detection. Civly sits on top of ACC, not against it.
- Say **IFC**, not "plugs into Revit." See [[Civly - Product and Tech]].
- Say **"Civly evaluates the alternatives you propose,"** not "Civly generates multiple drafts to choose from." See [[Civly - Design vs Engineering Decisions]].
- Lead with **3 LOIs plus the BOM pilot**, not the 5 users. See [[Civly - Traction and Funding]].
- Do not present Michael as a Strategic Advisor or partner publicly. Describe the relationship truthfully. See [[Civly - People]].

## The pitch is a founder-product-fit device
The origin arc (Leo's frustration, then Michael's dream software, then "we built it") is a fit narrative, and that is a legitimate use. One guardrail: fit is strongest when it is checkable, so keep Michael described accurately inside that story rather than as a committed partner.

## Sources and how to cite
The project knowledge that was ingested:
- **`_chat.txt`** — the WhatsApp export (Yash and Hassaan, Oct 2025 to 8 Jun 2026). The richest source. The Michael meeting (22 Apr), Leo conversations, the architect-panel talk, pitch and application drafts all live inside this one file.
- **`info pt1`** and **`info pt2`** — Claude chats in the civly-context project that analyse two batches of ~20 images each. `info pt2` is where Michael's walk-back message is read.
- **Master Info doc** — short summary plus traction. Some of it is stale (calls Michael a partner, says 2 LOIs).
- **Civly_pitch_deck** — the deck.

When citing, name the source and date, for example: "(Yash, 23 Apr 2026)", "(Michael, 22 Apr meeting)", or "(info pt2, ~6 Jun 2026)". This keeps claims checkable.
