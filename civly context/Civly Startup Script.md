"And here is how it works under the hood. When a project comes in, Civly converts the CAD or Rhino geometry into a structured BIM model, runs structural load verification, computes the optimal MEP routing, and keeps a live feasibility panel running the whole time so the design never blows the budget. When the model is finalised, it produces a full decision audit for the engineer to review and sign off on. The human is always in the loop. And if you want alternatives, Civly generates multiple BIM drafts to choose from.
