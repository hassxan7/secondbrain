## Who They Are
Reuben Roy is Yash's entrepreneurship tutor and has several years of experience in or around major architectural practice BVN. He has worked on architecture competitions and described the path from competition brief through design development, consultant engagement, and BIM coordination.

## Why They Matter to Civly
**Category:** Industry Expert  
**Relevance: HIGH**

Reuben provides the architectural-side counterweight to Hesh's MEP/BIM perspective. He identified competition bids as a potentially stronger architecture wedge, clarified that architectural draftspersons enter after plans are settled, and warned that enterprise Revit workflows are slow and risky to displace. Crucially, after watching Civly's demo he judged it to be an engineering-consultant product, which supports continuing the current MEP vertical slice while separately validating the competition thesis. He can also potentially introduce Civly to BVN's technology leadership.
