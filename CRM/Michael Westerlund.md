## About

As an extremely motivated person, I'm a ’doer’ and a maker. I blend strategic leadership with a deep commitment to fostering collaboration and innovation. I have headed staff, led big teams and worked closely with senior management to shape architectural designs as well as strategies including optimizing workflows and digital tools to support our collective vision. My goal, as a leader, to empower every team member is mirrored in the structures and systems I develop, fostering a space for diverse perspectives and collective growth.  
  
With a career spanning skyscrapers, museums, hotels and public buildings—often complex or politically sensitive—I’ve honed an ability to connect the seemingly unrelated, bringing people and ideas together in ways that spark new opportunities. Known for showing up well-prepared and fostering fruitful partnerships, I prioritize creating an environment where teams feel united and inspired to excel. Nothing warms me more than a core feedback from former team members: ’Michael brings not only us, but the offices closer together’. All I did was supporting them in becoming and being even more ’just’ themselves.  
  
Let’s connect to explore, together, how urban development can drive meaningful change, bringing us closer to a more empathic tomorrow!
