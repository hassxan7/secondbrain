## About

My career has taken me across luxury hospitality, business openings, workforce transformation and executive leadership - but the common thread has always been understanding how people drive performance. Get the people foundation right, and the business can scale. Get it wrong, and nothing else compensates.  
  
Some of what that's produced: turnover reduced from 75% to 19%. Engagement lifted from 6.9 to 8.3. Recruitment spend cut by 80% through talent attraction built on brand, not spend.  
  
I work at the intersection of strategy and operations. I've built HR functions from scratch, led transformations in businesses with 1,000+ employees, and navigated the full complexity of people leadership - from psychological injury management and SafeWork, FairWork, DHA matters to executive coaching and workforce redeployment. What I bring isn't just technical capability - it's judgment developed through doing the hard work in real environments, not consulting rooms.  
  
Most people experience HR as a set of rules. I've spent my career making it a competitive advantage.  
  
Lately I've been deep in a question that matters more than most organisations realise - how we use AI in a way that doesn't cost us our humanity. Not as a theoretical exercise, but practically: what does it look like day to day in a business, in a team, in a culture? In hospitality especially, where human connection is the product, that question isn't abstract. It's the next frontier of people leadership.  
  
I've also recently launched Roast My Resume - an AI tool helping people understand how they're actually being perceived in the job market and take back control of their careers.  
  
If you're thinking seriously about culture, employee experience, or what it takes to build a people function that actually performs - let's connect.
