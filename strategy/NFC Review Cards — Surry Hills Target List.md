Curated door-knock list for the Google-review NFC card side hustle. Compiled 4 Jul 2026 for the Saturday walk with Yash. **Not a Civly workstream** — this is the separate NFC/review-card business (the cards Hassaan bought 2 Jul).

**Product being sold:** small NFC card at ~$40–70 that a customer taps at the counter → auto-opens the business's Google review page. Reference product: [thereviewguys.com.au](https://thereviewguys.com.au/collections/frontpage) (they charge ~$70).

**Target profile:** owner-operator on premises · high review dependency · physical exit-point where a card can live · single decision-maker who can say yes on the spot · not corporate/chain.

**Approach at the door:** short intro → "you know those tap-to-review cards popping up at cafés and gyms? we sell them, ~$X, we set it up in 5 minutes and it points straight at your Google reviews" → offer to demo on our phone → close or leave a card and follow up.

## 🚶 Saturday walk route

Start north end of Crown St (~265) and walk south to ~660. Zigzag one block east onto Riley/Bourke and one block west onto Elizabeth for the outliers. Single loop = ~25 of the businesses above.

**Order to knock:**
1. **Roll In and Out** (Kippax) — warm-up, queue lets you chat while waiting
2. Barbers on Crown in order — The Barberia (323) → Verve (433) → Master Lashes (511) → House of Handsome (585)
3. Eateries in between each barber stop
4. Shoe/tailor/phone repair as you loop back on Bourke/Oxford

## 📞 Pitch cheat-sheet

**Cold open (7 seconds):**
> "Hey — you know those tap-to-review cards popping up at cafés and gyms lately? We make them for local businesses. Takes 2 minutes to show you if you've got a sec."

**Demo (30 seconds):** tap phone on our card → your Google review page opens instantly. "You leave this on the counter, customers tap when they pay. We've seen shops go from 2 reviews a month to 20."

**Price anchor:** "$40 for the small ones, $70 for the countertop board. One-off, no subscription."

**Common objections:**
- *"We already have a QR code"* → "This is faster — no camera app, no scan, one tap. Older customers actually use it."
- *"How does it work technically?"* → "NFC chip inside, same tech as tap-to-pay. Every phone made in the last 8 years reads it."
- *"Let me think about it"* → leave a business card + demo card, note their name, follow up in 3 days.

**Ask for the sale:** "Want me to program one for you now?"

## 🔧 Follow-up: automated data pull

The [`outscraper`](https://pypi.org/project/outscraper) Python SDK is installed locally. With a free Outscraper API key (~500 credits on signup) we can pull real Place IDs, phone numbers, owner names, exact review counts, and email addresses — enough to send a proper follow-up SMS/email blast to whoever we can't close in person. See `github.com/jayozer/outscraper-mcp` for the MCP wrapper if we want Claude to query it live.
