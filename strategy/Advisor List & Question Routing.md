## Question-routing process

> **Owners:** Hassaan maintains the bench + matches questions to advisors and owns the **business relationships** (Michael, Hesh). **Yash runs the research interviews**, especially the **UNSW/academic channel** (Priscilla, Reuben Roy) — his contacts, his questions.

1. **Yash drafts the open questions** (technical + workflow) — see the live list below and [[To-Do — Yash]].
2. **Hassaan matches each question** to the best-fit advisor using the "Best for" column above and secures a line of contact.
3. **Yash runs the interview** with that advisor (he owns the UNSW/academic + technical-advisor conversations).
4. **If no advisor fits**, ask the nearest advisor to **introduce us to someone who can** (each advisor is also a connector). E.g. duct-sizing specifics → Hesh → UK MEP modeller.
5. **Keep a constant line of contact** per advisor (Hesh: monthly call + text; Michael: bi-weekly-ish informal; etc.) so questions don't pile up.
6. **Log answers** back into the relevant wiki page (concept/MEP/source) so knowledge compounds.
