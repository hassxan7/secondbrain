Builds on the [[Civly_Launch_Storyboard_VO|launch storyboard]] and respects [[Civly - Messaging Rules and Open Questions|the messaging rules]].

## The mindset fix (read this first)
Hassaan's instinct is to not post until something is concrete. For a startup that instinct is the enemy. Reframe:

- **Posting is distribution, not a victory lap.** Every person you send a connection request to checks your profile and the company page. A dead feed makes a cold outreach colder. An active feed makes the same message convert.
- **Build-in-public compounds.** Followers, credibility, and inbound accrue from *consistency*, not from one perfect post. The cost of a post is ~20 minutes; the cost of waiting is months of silence while competitors (Snaptrude, Archilabs) own the narrative.
- **You already have plenty concrete:** 3 LOIs, Blackbird Giants, a working demo, advisor conversations, a real pivot story. That is more than 95% of founders posting daily.
- **Rule of thumb:** if you learned something this week or moved one inch, that is a post. Progress is the content.

## Content pillars (rotate these)
1. **Build-in-public progress** — what we shipped/learned this week, wins and stumbles. (Highest trust.)
2. **Industry insight / education** — teach what we are learning about BIM, LOD, the 2-to-4-month bottleneck, why drafting is so manual. Positions us as people who *get it*.
3. **Product / demos** — short clips of the agent doing one thing well. The prompt-bar moment.
4. **Founder POV / contrarian** — the protein-folding analogy, "Cursor for architects", why AI cannot be trusted with code unless it is deterministic.
5. **Social proof / milestones** — LOIs, Blackbird Giants, advisor conversations, pilot interest. (Lead with the 3 LOIs, never the old "5 users" number.)

## Cadence
- **3 posts/week** to start (sustainable), from **Hassaan** and **Yash** personal profiles + reposted on the **Civly company page**. Founders' personal reach beats the company page early, so post as people first.
- Engage daily for 10 minutes (comment on architects', BIM, and proptech posts) — this is how the outreach audience finds you.

## Format / hook tips
- **First line is the whole game.** Hook with a claim or question; no "I'm excited to announce."
- **Readable with sound off**, captions on every video.
- **One idea per post.** Short lines, whitespace.
- **End with a soft CTA** (comment a keyword, DM for access, link in comments — links in comments, not the body, for reach).
- Lead carousels/videos with the transformation, the logo comes after the magic (per the storyboard).

## Profile / page setup (do once)
- **Founders' headlines:** e.g. "Co-founder, Civly | AI that drafts your BIM model from a sketch". Fix the LinkedIn **"Enngineers" typo**.
- **Civly company page:** clear one-liner, banner, link; fix **SEO** so "Civly" surfaces (Yash, noted 14 Jun — Snaptrude's site is the benchmark for clarity).
- **Pin** the launch video once live.

## Guardrails (from the messaging rules)
- **No em dashes** in any copy. Say **IFC**, not "plugs into Revit." Lead with the **3 LOIs**, not "5 users." Do **not** present Michael as a formal advisor publicly. Show control via the **approval mechanic** ("you approve every decision"), do not say "you stay in control." Frame as "fastest way to a BIM draft", not a coordination tool.

## Metrics to watch
Followers, post engagement rate, profile views (spikes after outreach), inbound DMs, waitlist signups, and how many outreach replies mention "saw your post". The last one proves the loop between content and the [[Data Partnership Outreach Strategy|outreach funnel]].
