## TL;DR

Career guide for architects and engineers on how to learn Revit quickly and position as a BIM expert. The presenter went from uni graduate to BIM coordinator in 6 months by learning Revit foundations first — then transitioned into project management. Main thesis: 95% of people waste time on the wrong Revit features; focus on coordination-ready skills and showcase them to employers.

## Key Claims / Techniques

- Revit is the foundational skill that unlocks all AEC career paths (coordinator → manager → PM)
- Most people learn Revit for 6+ months via courses but learn the wrong things (too academic, not industry-applicable)
- BIM coordination is the natural next step after 6 months of Revit modelling
- The presenter handled projects totalling ~$500M–$1B in the first 6–12 months of their career, including hospitals, prisons, schools, luxury apartments
- Key market context: AEC firms want people who can demonstrate practical coordination skills, not just modelling
- Showcasing skills (portfolio, GitHub-style proof of work) matters more than credentials
