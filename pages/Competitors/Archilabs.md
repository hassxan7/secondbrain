## Built different. Actually.

The best of both worlds: AI speed for modeling, sheets, annotations, and documentation, combined with deterministic validation so project teams can move faster without losing control.

### Smart Components

Components carry their own intelligence. A rack knows its power draw, clearance rules, and cooling requirements. Move it, validate, and the platform checks every dependency in seconds.

### Python-Native Automation

The entire platform is built around Python as a first-class interface. Components are Python classes. Automation workflows are Python scripts. AI can generate them from plain English.

### AI Speed, Deterministic Results

AI generates and iterates designs at speed, but every workflow is deterministic, auditable, and reproducible. You get faster delivery without sacrificing the reliability your projects demand.

### Version Control for Designs

Every change is tracked. Branch a layout, explore alternatives, diff changes, merge back. Multiple people work simultaneously without file locking or merge conflicts.

### End-to-End BIM/Design Workspace

Import drawings, create structured models, automate sheets and annotations, generate schedules and documentation packages, and export back into the BIM stack your team already uses.

### Proactive Error Prevention

Validation is computed, not manual. Workflows produce typed violations with severity and root-cause grouping. Design errors are caught in the platform, not on the construction site.

## From idea to validated design

The workflow that turns manual modeling, annotation, sheet setup, and documentation work into guided, AI-assisted design delivery.

01

### Describe your intent

Tell the AI what you want — in plain English or Python. Place racks, route ductwork, size electrical distribution. The platform understands your design vocabulary.

02

### AI generates the workflow

ArchiLabs translates intent into executable Recipes — versioned, deterministic workflows that are fully auditable. Preview everything before it runs. Full undo at every step.

03

### Validate automatically

Smart components check themselves. Power budgets, cooling capacity, clearance violations, redundancy policies — all validated in seconds, not hours.

04

### Document and ship

Branch layouts, merge the winner, then generate sheets, annotations, schedules, reports, and model exports for the BIM and design tools your team already uses.
